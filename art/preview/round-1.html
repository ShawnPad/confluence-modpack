Confluence Textures

Every texture the pack owns: items at 1× and 8× in an inventory slot, blocks as a tile. Leave a comment on anything; the PNGs live in kubejs/assets/kubejs/textures/ and the rules in art/STYLE.md.

The pack ships tiers 0-2 today: the coin and the Twilight Key are in the game. Everything tagged (v0.2), (v0.3) or (v1.0) is designed but not built yet; the tag is the release it lands in. A "tier" is one rung of the pack's ladder of gated dimensions. The current PNGs are a baseline, not a constraint: redraw anything, keeping only the canvas rules (STYLE.md rules 1-3) and each family's shared silhouette (rule 6).

One-offs

Confluence Catalyst
kubejs:confluence_catalyst

The pack's final item, to be made at the Ars Nouveau Enchanting Apparatus; every mod's creative-tier item will be rewritten to consume one (v1.0).

Confluence Coin
kubejs:coin

The questbook currency, in the game today. Every quest pays a few; the Coin Shop chapter sells repeatable reward bags for them.

Keys - the two dimension keys the pack adds (the other tiers reuse existing items); each is used up opening its dimension

Twilight Key
kubejs:twilight_key

In the game today (tier 2). The only thing that opens a Twilight Forest portal in this pack; the diamond trigger is disabled. Thrown into the water pool like the vanilla diamond, one key per portal.

Key to The Other
kubejs:other_key

Will be consumed at an Allthemodium Teleport Pad placed in the Nether to reach The Other, the tier-5 dimension (v0.3).

Foci - Ars Nouveau Enchanting Apparatus products that gate the Other key and the catalyst

End Focus
kubejs:end_focus

The tier-4 magic composite: an Enchanting Apparatus product that will be an input of the Other key (v0.2).

Astral Focus
kubejs:astral_focus

The last focus: the magic-side input of the Confluence Catalyst, and the reason the two planet ores exist (v0.3).

Shards - drops of the two planet ores, the Astral Focus inputs

Mercury Shard
kubejs:mercury_shard

Dropped by Mercury Ore (the block already drops it); one of the two Astral Focus inputs, and nothing else will make it.

Glacio Shard
kubejs:glacio_shard

Dropped by Glacio Ore (the block already drops it); the other Astral Focus input, and nothing else will make it.

Ores - the two pack blocks, one on Mercury and one on Glacio

Mercury Ore
kubejs:mercury_ore

Pack block that will generate only in Mercury's one biome, buried in small veins (v0.3; not placed yet). Drops Mercury Shards.

Glacio Ore
kubejs:glacio_ore

Pack block that will generate only in Glacio's two biomes, buried in small veins (v0.3; not placed yet). Drops Glacio Shards.

Blocks are shown as a 2×2 tile at 4× so seams show. Items sit in a vanilla-style slot at 8× with the 1× original beside them. Generated by tools/gen_preview.py from art/briefs.json.
