# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- Tiers 5–6 are playable: Overworld → Nether → Twilight Forest → Undergarden → The End → **The Other** → **Space** (Moon, Mars, Venus, Mercury, Glacio), plus the **Eternal Starlight** spur.
- **The Other (tier 5).** Key to The Other: End Focus, Vibranium ingot, IF Advanced Machine Frame, Essence of the Bees, dragon egg. Right-click a Teleport Pad placed in the Nether while holding the key (main hand or off hand) to unlock The Other for your whole FTB team; one key is used up. Then sneak and right-click the pad with both hands empty to travel. Until a team unlocks it, its players cannot teleport in (commands such as `/tpa` and `/home`, waystones, the Mekanism teleporter, spells), and anyone who still arrives, for example through a Tombstone tablet, is sent to the world spawn. Creative and spectator players are exempt from those two checks, not from the pad. The Teleport Pad in the End no longer opens The Beyond: it is closed.
- **Worldgen.** Unobtainium ore generates in The Other, in cave floors of ancient stone between y 8 and 128, in all seven of its biomes (about one ore per chunk). Mercury ore generates on Mercury and Glacio ore on Glacio. Allthemodium's own Unobtainium placement stays off. **Chunks of The Other, Mercury and Glacio generated before 0.3.0 have none of these ores; explore new chunks.**
- **Tier-5 recipes.** Ultimate Control Circuit: 4 elite circuits, 2 atomic alloy, 2 Unobtainium ingots and a Wilden Tribute make 4; Mekanism's own two recipes (the crafting-table one, 2 atomic alloy and an elite circuit, and the infusing one) are gone. Supreme Machine Frame (Dissolution Chamber): Advanced frame, 2 plastic, a nitro crystal, an ultimate circuit, 2 Unobtainium ingots, an Unobtainium - Allthemodium alloy ingot, 135 mB ether gas. The three Allthemodium alloys take one Piglich Heart each: Unobtainium - Allthemodium (Enchanting Apparatus, 10,000 source: Unobtainium reagent; Allthemodium ingot, the heart and the four elemental essences), Vibranium - Allthemodium (Energizing Orb, 1,000,000,000 FE: Allthemodium and Vibranium ingots, the heart, a nitro crystal block), Unobtainium - Vibranium (Dissolution Chamber: 4 pink slime, Vibranium and Unobtainium ingots, the heart, 100 mB soul lava). AE2's Inscriber, Charger, ME Controller, ME Drive, ME Import and Export Bus and ME Terminal use Ozone Skyblock Reborn 2's recipes (Supreme frame, knightmetal, a forgotten ingot, QIO parts). The Digital Miner takes an ultimate circuit.
- **Space (tier 6).** Nasa Workbench: an ultimate circuit, an Unobtainium - Allthemodium alloy ingot, a block of Unobtainium and an Unrealium ingot, in place of its iron rods and redstone torches. Space Suit: plus an elytra. Oxygen Loader: an electric pump in place of the redstone block. Fuel Refinery: the four elemental essences, an ultimate circuit and a Vibranium - Allthemodium alloy ingot. Steel Engine: a nitro crystal, an ultimate circuit and an Unobtainium - Vibranium alloy ingot. Rockets, the launch pad and the desh, ostrum and calorite engines keep their recipes; each engine is built from the one before it. Etrium Ingot: Etrionic Blast Furnace, one desh, one ostrum and one calorite ingot and an ice shard; Etrium Plate by compressing. Astral Focus (Enchanting Apparatus): Etrium ingot reagent; Divine Soulshard, Mercury shard, Glacio shard; no source. AE2: the Wireless Receiver takes desh; the Crafting Unit and the ME Pattern Provider take ostrum; the Molecular Assembler and the ME Pattern Encoding Terminal keep their recipes.
- **Bypasses.** Unobtainium's 5× Mekanism chain (ore, raw ore, raw block → dirty slurry) is replaced by Combiner recipes that also use an Etrium Ingot (5, 10 and 30 Unobtainium crystals). Drygmies cannot farm the Piglich, the Echo of Tyros or The Dead King. New Tombstone Lost Tablets lead only to structures in the dimension where they wake (`config/tombstone-common.toml`: `lost_tablet_search_outside_world = false`).
- **Changes to 0.2 play.** Tools below Vibranium tier, for example the Forgotten and Resonarium pickaxes, the Infernal Forge, the Atomic Disassembler and the Meka-Tool, no longer mine Vibranium or Unobtainium ore, as the tree always intended; they still mine Allthemodium ore. A Wither, or Cataclysm's Ignis, Maledictus, Scylla or Netherite Monstrosity, no longer breaks Allthemodium, Vibranium or Unobtainium ore. Allthemodium, Vibranium and Unobtainium ore and raw metal no longer smelt in a plain furnace. That also stops the Mekanism Flamethrower and the AE2 Entropy Manipulator cooking them, and Mekanism's Energized Smelter and smelting factories, which use furnace recipes. A blast furnace still smelts them, and their dusts still smelt in all of these. The QIO Drive Array, QIO Drive, QIO Importer, QIO Exporter and QIO Redstone Adapter, the Quantum Entangloporter and the Chemical Dissolution Chamber, Washer and Crystallizer take elite circuits instead of ultimate ones (cheaper, same tier). The ultimate circuit and everything built from it (ultimate factories, induction provider, MekaSuit, Meka-Tool, fusion, SPS, the Antiprotonic Nucleosynthesizer, the Ultimate Bin) and the Digital Miner now wait for tier 5. AE2's machines and Ad Astra's workbench, suit, oxygen loader, refinery and steel engine used their default recipes in 0.2; anything already built keeps working.
- **Questbook.** Three new chapters (The Other, Space, Eternal Starlight; 14 chapters, 112 quests). The Key to The Other and the Nasa Workbench give an Epic bag, and reaching Mercury gives a Legendary bag; the Coin Shop sells them for 192 coins (after the key) and 384 coins (after Mercury), three items per bag. Shipped quests corrected: a second Essence of the Bees comes from resetting the Bee Queen; Productive Bees does have tier-metal bees, and each needs its metal first; Unobtainium's ore chain needs Etrium; the Drygmy list; the ME Controller wants one forgotten ingot; the elite-circuit machines; flight before the End.
- **Fixes and small changes.** Bows and crossbows can be enchanted again (books at an anvil and the enchanting table); Allthemodium's dead tag entries were removing them from the enchantable list. Loot bags are now named Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary Bag instead of "Loot Crate". Raw tier ore quests now say to use a blast furnace or turn it into dust. Supplementaries' recipes and worldgen tags could silently vanish on about 1 in 10 starts, and a rare start-up crash was the same race; both are fixed by loading mods in a fixed order on one thread. Twilight Forest's game rules could go missing the same way, and a start-up guard now registers them first. The server reads Ad Astra's radio station list from a copy in the pack, so it no longer depends on a website at start-up.
- **Your `config/fml.toml`.** This update replaces your `config/fml.toml` once. If you changed it (for example `earlyWindowControl` for graphics problems), re-apply your change afterwards; it will stick until the pack changes the file again.
- **Known issue.** On Apple Silicon Macs, generating new terrain can rarely crash a world or stop terrain loading in that dimension until a restart. It comes from Minecraft's own lighting code, not the pack, and did not reproduce on the x86 server.
- **Tooling.** `check_quests.py` covers tiers 5–6 and keeps tier-5/6 key inputs, boss drops and planet materials out of every reward table; worldgen rows take a per-row `discard` and a list of biomes; the worldgen rows and the pad and teleport-check logic have node-run tests under pytest.

## 0.2.0
- Tiers 3–4 are playable: Overworld → Nether → Twilight Forest → Undergarden → The End. New gates: Undergarden Catalyst (knightmetal + Allthemodium + IF Simple Machine Frame + Iron's Upgrade Orb), Powah nitro crystal (six inputs incl. spirited crystal, arcane essence, uraninite), Mekanism elite control circuit (+ Allthemodium ingot) and refined obsidian ingot (Combiner: dust + Allthemodium nugget), Ars Ritual Brazier (+ spirited crystal + second source gem block), Iron's Lightning Upgrade Orb (7 runes + orb + Allthemodium), QIO dashboard / drive array / importer / exporter (Vibranium), Eye of Ender (Vibranium + forgotten ingot + Resonarium + nitro + Ritual Brazier; the vanilla and Mystical Agriculture recipes are gone), IF Advanced Machine Frame (2 Vibranium + Lightning Upgrade Orb replace the netherite scrap and one gold), Ars Planarium (+ teleportation core + refined obsidian) and the pack's End Focus (Apparatus: Planarium + 4 essences + dragon's breath + Gauntlet of Guard + Ritual Brazier + source gem block).
- Worldgen: Vibranium ore now generates only in Undergarden cave floors between y −55 and −16 (about one per chunk, measured 1.2/chunk over five 256-chunk squares). **Undergarden chunks generated before 0.2.0 have no Vibranium.** The Vibranium upgrade smithing template rides in Undergarden catacombs chests at 2/14 (about one chest in seven; measured 45 in 300 rolls).
- Bypasses: the Vibranium 5× Mekanism chain (ore / raw / raw block → dirty slurry) is replaced by Combiner recipes that also consume an Unobtainium ingot, so ore quintupling waits for tier 5. Drygmies cannot farm the Forgotten Guardian, Sludges, Stalkers, the Ender Guardian, the Netherite Monstrosity, the Ender Dragon, the Wither, the Warden or the Bee Queen (`ars_nouveau:drygmy_blacklist`).
- Questbook: four new chapters (The Undergarden, The End, The Otherside, The Bumblezone; 11 chapters, 73 quests). Ore Quintupling now also waits on Vibranium. Coin Shop: Rare bag for 96 coins after the Undergarden Catalyst (netherite, diamonds, emeralds, steel, energized steel, ender pearls, blaze rods, coins), repeatable.
- Tooling: the chapter scaffolder renders `advancement:` tasks and refuses `repeat: true` on tasks that complete by themselves; `tools/check_tags.py` knows block and biome tags; loot injection goes through `addToPool`, which logs and skips instead of silently landing in the wrong pool.

## 0.1.3
- Fix: the questbook showed "Unnamed Group" / "Unnamed" for every group, chapter and quest, with no descriptions. The translations sat in `lang/en_us/{chapter,chapter_group}.snbt` + `lang/en_us/chapters/*.snbt`, the layout ATM10 uses - but ATM10 ships the FTB Quests Lang Splitter mod to merge those files, and FTB Quests itself reads only a flat `lang/en_us.snbt`. The pack now ships that one file (same keys, same text). Server-side and client-side; relaunch picks it up.
- Fix: the Allthemodium upgrade smithing template was in Twilight stronghold caches at weight 1 against a pool of weight 575 (1 chest in 576). It is now weight 45, about 1 cache in 14 (measured 17 in 300 rolls). And the template no longer appears where Allthemodium itself put it: Ancient City suspicious clay (`allthemodium:arch`) and, for the Vibranium template, bastion suspicious soul sand (`allthemodium:arch2`) now roll empty. Chests and suspicious blocks generated before this update keep their old contents.
- Quest text rewritten for players: no more tree ids or "Rewritten:"/"Audit:" prefixes; each description says what to do and what it leads to. Checkmark quests keep their 3 coins.
- Prism instance zip: the instance now asks for a 6 GB heap (`OverrideMemory`, `MaxMemAlloc=6144`). A single-player world on Prism's default 4 GB crashed with `java.lang.OutOfMemoryError: Java heap space`. Instances imported earlier: set 6144 MiB by hand once (PLAYTEST.md, Setup).
- Tooling: `tools/probe_loot.sh` rolls a loot table into a chest N times on the headless server and counts an item (the only check that sees what LootJS did to a table); `tools/scaffold_chapter.py --lang-only` rebuilds the lang file from `tools/chapters/*.yaml`; `check_quests.py` maps tree nodes to quests through their deterministic ids instead of a node-id line in the description.
- Verified, not changed: uraninite generates in the Mining Dimension at about 110 ore per chunk between y 65 and 250 (`tools/probe_worldgen.sh`, fresh 256-chunk block); a tester looked below y 0. PLAYTEST D1 now says where to look.

## 0.1.2
- Fix: Allthemodium never generated in the Twilight Forest (0 ore in 511 freshly generated chunks). The placement rule, copied from Allthemodium's own Deep Dark file, only starts in `minecraft:air` and aims at cave ceilings; the Twilight's caves are carver `cave_air`, and an ore vein sits at or below its origin, so the rule placed nothing. Veins now generate in the **floors** of Twilight caves below y 10, about one vein every 2-3 chunks (measured 1.1 ore per chunk over 256 chunks). Server-side: only chunks generated after the restart have ore, so explore Twilight caves nobody has visited yet. Quest text and PLAYTEST rows updated; the Mining Dimension never had Allthemodium in this pack (the 0.1.0 note below was wrong - it has uraninite only).
- Tooling: `tools/probe_worldgen.sh` boots the local server, generates a fresh block of chunks in one dimension and counts blocks in a y band.
